What to include, by type of message

None of this is required — a one-line message is fine and we will ask if we need more. But if you already have the details to hand, including them usually saves a round trip and gets the problem fixed in the first reply rather than the third.

A tool is behaving wrongly

The most useful thing by far is input that reproduces it — with anything sensitive replaced by dummy values that still trigger the bug. Add which tool, what you expected, what happened instead, and your browser and version. Most parser bugs come down to one specific character in one specific position, and without a sample we are guessing.

A guide has an error

Please tell us. Technical writing that is wrong is worse than no writing at all, and we would rather correct it than leave it standing. Quote the sentence and say what it should say — a specification reference is welcome but not necessary. Corrections get made and the page's modified date updated.

A security issue

Put [SECURITY] in the subject line and describe the issue and how to reproduce it. Since the tools run entirely in your browser and we hold no accounts or user data, the realistic surface is things like a cross-site scripting vector in how a tool renders your input — which we treat seriously and fix first.

A tool that should exist

Tell us the task, not just the tool name. "I keep having to diff two JSON objects and ignore key order" tells us far more than "add a JSON diff", because the real requirement is usually narrower and more specific than the generic tool would be.

Engineering work

A paragraph on what you are trying to achieve, roughly when you need it, and any fixed constraints — existing stack, systems it must integrate with, compliance requirements. We will tell you honestly whether it is something we are a good fit for, including when the answer is no.

Using a tool in your business

You do not need our permission to use the tools for commercial work — they are free for that, and no attribution is required. Do get in touch if you need something beyond that, such as a self-hosted copy running inside your own network.

Who you are contacting

Layerbit Technologies is an independent software studio based in the United Arab Emirates. It builds and maintains the free browser-based developer tools on this site, and takes on client engineering work — API automation, ERP systems, application development, static site delivery and portfolio design.

There is no call centre and no ticketing system. Messages go to the person who writes the code, which means replies are not instant but they are informed. If you are asking why a particular tool made a particular decision about your data, you will be answered by whoever made that decision.

For how the site handles data and cookies, see our privacy policy. For the terms covering use of the tools, see the terms of service. Background on how and why the site is built the way it is lives on the about page.
