My day job is banking software: I am a senior software analyst in Dubai, supporting and upgrading the payment and clearing applications a UAE digital bank runs on. The guides on this site cover the formats and failure modes that come up in that kind of work every day: exports that break in Excel, SQL that has to behave on both Oracle and SQL Server, scheduled jobs, time zones, tokens and integration messages. Outside work I build Android apps and websites under the Layerbit name.

How the guides are written

Each guide starts from a problem that comes up in real systems and is checked against the specification it depends on, such as RFC 4180 for CSV, RFC 7519 for JWTs or the cron manual. If you spot a mistake, please write to me and I will correct the page. More about the site itself, and how it handles your data, is on the About Layerbit page.
