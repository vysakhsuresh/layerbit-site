Kshana App Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026. This policy covers Kshana, the daily-guidance app for Android (package com.kshana.app). It is separate from the general Layerbit website privacy policy, which covers this website's own advertising, analytics and cookies; none of that applies to the app, which contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and sets no cookies. In short: every reading is worked out on your phone. Kshana has no account, no server and no advertising, and the developer receives none of your data.

What the app keeps, and where

Everything below stays on your phone, in the app's own private storage:

  • Your birth details: the date, time and place you enter, and how sure you are of the time.
  • The people you add: their birth details and how they are related to you, including other people who use the app on the same phone.
  • Your journal, chat and records: questions you ask, events you note, whether past readings came true, and your remedy ticks and streaks.
  • Your settings: language, place, reminders and similar choices.

The backup file

So that an uninstall or a cleared phone does not lose your data, Kshana saves a copy of your own data as a file on your phone at Downloads/Kshana/kshana-backup.json. You can turn this off in Settings, and you can export or import a backup yourself. Nothing is uploaded; the file stays on your phone unless you move or share it. Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this app.

Internet

Readings never need the internet. The app's online features are off by default and work only after you switch on Settings › Online. Even then, nothing is sent unless you use one of these features:

  • Place search: the place name you type is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find its location. Only the words you typed are sent.
  • Earth-rotation data: the app downloads a public data file from the International Earth Rotation Service. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Narration (bring your own key): if you add your own Anthropic API key and ask for a narration, your reading (including the birth details it was worked out from) and your question are sent to Anthropic over an encrypted connection, under your own account and Anthropic's policy. The key is stored only on your phone.

The developer runs no server and receives none of this.

On-device wording: on phones that have Google's built-in Gemini Nano, the chat's English answers can be reworded on the phone. Your words stay on the phone. Google's ML Kit library that does this may send Google technical details such as the phone model, Android version and how fast the feature ran, to keep it working. It does not send your words or readings.

Links you choose to open, such as a charity's donation page or a UPI payment app, open in those apps under their own policies.

Permissions, and why

  • Notifications: to show the reminders and the sunrise reading you turn on.
  • Alarms and reminders (optional): to ring at the exact minute you chose. Without it, reminders still come, a few minutes late at most.
  • Calendar, read only (optional): only if you turn on the calendar check. The app reads the next days' events on the phone to point out ones that fall in a difficult stretch. It never stores, changes or sends your calendar.
  • Microphone (optional): only when you tap voice ask. Your phone's own speech service turns your speech into text, and the app keeps no recording.
  • Start at boot: to set your chosen reminders again after the phone restarts.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, the error is written to a file inside the app on your phone. It is never sent automatically. You can read it in Settings and share it only if you choose to.

Deleting your data

Everything Kshana keeps is on your phone. Remove a person in the app, clear the app's data in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Also delete Downloads/Kshana/kshana-backup.json if you no longer want the backup copy.

Children's privacy

Kshana is meant for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the date at the top. Kshana will not send anything new off the phone without this policy saying so first.
