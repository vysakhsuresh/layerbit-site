Apps and Websites Built by Layerbit

Beyond the developer tools on this site, Layerbit makes Android apps and websites. Every one of them is built by founder Vysakh Suresh, and every one follows the same rule: what you create stays on your own device.

Android apps

All seven apps are released on Google Play, and each one's privacy policy is public and linked below.

Screen sharing

LayerLink

Broadcast your Android screen to anyone's web browser in real time. Start a broadcast, share the link, and the other person watches in an ordinary browser, with nothing to install and no account on either side.

  • Video travels directly between the two devices over WebRTC
  • A signalling service only helps the devices find each other; it never carries video
  • Viewers open the stream with the LayerLink Viewer on this site
Privacy policy
Video editor

Squish

A video editor for your phone: put clips, photos, music and captions together into a finished video and export it to your gallery. It works fully offline.

  • Videos, photos, sound and projects never leave the phone
  • A few optional online extras, such as caption translation and free stock footage, stay off until you switch them on
  • No advertising or analytics code inside the app
Privacy policy
Photo editor

Still

A photo editor built so that your pictures physically cannot leave your phone: Still does not ask Android for internet access at all, so the app has no way to open a network connection.

  • No internet permission, which you can check on the Play listing
  • No account, no ads, no tracking
  • Clearing the app's storage removes everything it kept
Privacy policy
Audio studio

Stave

An audio studio for your phone: record, edit and transcribe, build beats, and use the tuner and live voice effects. Like Still, Stave has no internet permission, so your recordings stay on the device.

  • No internet permission at all
  • The microphone opens only when you ask it to, with Android’s indicator showing
  • No account, no ads, no tracking
Privacy policy
Money tracker

Penny

Know what is safe to spend. Penny keeps your accounts, transactions, budgets, bills and goals in one place, encrypted on your phone.

  • Data is encrypted on the device
  • Sync between devices is optional and end-to-end encrypted
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data
Privacy policy
IPO tracker

IPO Radar

Every Indian IPO in the pipeline, mainboard and SME, with its dates, grey-market premium and subscription figures. For each one it estimates the chance of a listing-day gain, the likely gain range, your odds of being allotted shares, and what one application is really worth after charges and tax.

  • A plain verdict, from apply to avoid, with the warnings behind it
  • Each prediction is locked when bidding closes and later checked against the real listing
  • No account, ads or tracking; it only downloads public data files. Information, not investment advice
Privacy policy
Daily guidance

Kshana

A daily-guidance app whose readings are worked out on your phone. It keeps a journal of the questions you ask and lets you note whether past readings came true, with reminders you set yourself.

  • No account and no server; online features are opt-in
  • Your data is backed up to a file on your own phone, which you can switch off
  • No advertising or analytics code inside the app
Privacy policy

Websites

foolscap.co.in

  • Includes a developer roadmap, interview preparation, a glossary and a cheat sheet
  • Progress is saved in your own browser; no sign-up
dotnetmadeeasy.com

  • Available in Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil and English

Guides and tool reference

How everything here is built

Your work stays on your device. The browser tools and websites process what you type inside the page, and the apps keep your photos, recordings, settings and money data on the phone. Two of the apps, Still and Stave, go further and do not ask Android for internet access at all, so they could not send anything even by mistake.

No accounts. None of the apps or websites asks you to sign up. Where something needs to sync, as Penny's optional sync does, it is encrypted on your phone before it leaves.

No advertising or tracking inside the apps. None of the seven apps contains an advertising or analytics SDK. This website carries Google ads, which is how the free tools are paid for, and its privacy policy says exactly what they collect.

Plain-English privacy policies. Every app has a public policy that says what stays on the phone, what can leave it and why, written to be read rather than skimmed past.
