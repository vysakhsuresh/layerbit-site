Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026. This policy explains exactly what happens to the data you type into Layerbit's tools, what the site collects about your visit, and which third parties are involved. We have tried to be specific rather than reassuring — where a guarantee is strong we say why, and where it is weaker we say that too.

The short version

Almost every tool on this site processes your input entirely inside your own browser. Nothing you paste into the JSON validator, the JWT decoder, the SQL formatter or any other local tool is transmitted to us or to anyone else, because there is no endpoint receiving it. The site does serve advertising and runs an anonymous visitor counter, both of which set cookies, and a consent notice appears before that happens.

Local tools: your input never leaves your device

The following tools run as client-side JavaScript in the page you have open: JSON Validator & Formatter, Universal SQL Formatter, JWT Decoder & Verifier, QR / Barcode Generator, Base64 Encode / Decode, JSON Table Viewer, Text Diff Checker, CSV ↔ JSON Converter, YAML ↔ JSON Converter, Cron Expression Generator, URL Encoder / Decoder, Log Analyzer, Regex Tester, HTML Escape / Unescape, and the UAE PINT-AE Compliance Checker.

For all of these, the text, code or files you provide are read, processed and displayed by your own browser. They are not uploaded, not logged, and not retained by us — we never receive them in the first place. You do not have to take this on trust: open your browser's developer tools, switch to the Network tab, and use any of these tools; nothing leaves. You can also load a tool, disconnect from the internet, and continue using it normally.

Because we never receive this data, we cannot disclose it, sell it, lose it in a breach, or hand it over on request. That is a property of how the site is built rather than a promise about our intentions.

LayerLink: the one exception

LayerLink screen sharing is badged Cloud API on the homepage because it cannot work purely locally — two browsers have no way to find each other without help. It uses WebRTC, which means:

  • The video stream travels directly between the two participants' browsers and is encrypted in transit using DTLS-SRTP, which WebRTC mandates.
  • A signalling service (Google Firebase Realtime Database) carries only the connection negotiation data the two browsers need in order to locate each other — session descriptions and candidate network addresses. It does not carry video, and we do not record or store the contents of a session.
  • Session identifiers are transient. Once a session ends, the identifier is spent.

Anyone holding a session identifier can attempt to join a live session, so treat a LayerLink link like a temporary password and share it only over a channel you trust.

What the site collects about your visit

We do not operate an application server, a user database, or any account system. There is no sign-up and no login anywhere on Layerbit, so there is no profile associated with you. The data that is collected comes from three places:

  • Advertising (Google AdSense). Google serves ads on this site and, in doing so, may collect and process data including your IP address, device and browser characteristics, and information about your visit, and may set or read cookies. This is governed by Google's own privacy policy rather than ours.
  • Anonymous visitor counter (Google Firebase). The homepage shows a running count of sessions. It increments a single shared number and records nothing that identifies an individual visitor — no addresses, no identifiers, no per-visit records.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us or message us on WhatsApp, we hold that correspondence in order to reply to it. We do not add you to a mailing list and there is nothing to unsubscribe from.

To be explicit about a common assumption: at the time of writing this site does not run Google Analytics or any other analytics package. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships, and the consent notice will cover it.

Cookies and your choices

A notice appears on your first visit explaining that the site uses cookies for advertising and, on the tools that require it, for core functionality. Accepting it stores a single flag in your browser's local storage so the notice does not reappear — that flag stays on your device and is never sent to us.

You have several independent controls:

  • Personalised advertising can be turned off at Google My Ad Center, which affects Google ads across every site, not just this one.
  • Cookies generally can be blocked or cleared in your browser settings. Doing so does not affect your ability to use any local tool on this site — they need no cookies to function.
  • Advertising entirely can be blocked with a content blocker. We would rather you used the tools with an ad blocker than not used them.

Your rights over your data

Depending on where you live, data protection law may give you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to receive a copy of it. We support all of these — and in practice the answer is usually short, because there is very little to act on.

For the local tools there is nothing to request, access or delete: we hold none of it. For correspondence you have sent us, email ceo@layerbit.co.in and we will provide a copy or delete it. For data held by Google in connection with advertising, requests need to go to Google, since they are the controller for that processing and we have no ability to access or delete it on your behalf.

Security

Every page is served over HTTPS. The site is entirely static — pre-rendered HTML with no application server and no database — which removes most of the categories of vulnerability that lead to data disclosure, for the simple reason that the systems which would be attacked do not exist. The correspondence we do hold is kept in a standard email account protected by multi-factor authentication.

Children's privacy

Layerbit is a developer tools site intended for a professional and technical audience. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the date at the top. Material changes — a new third-party service, a new category of data collected — will be reflected here before the change is deployed, not afterwards. In September 2026 we removed three tools that queried third-party APIs for breach data, URL reputation and IP geolocation; this policy no longer references them, and no data from those tools was retained.

Contact

Questions about this policy, requests about your data, or anything you think is inaccurate here: ceo@layerbit.co.in. If something in this document does not match what the site actually does, we would genuinely like to know — please tell us and we will correct it.
