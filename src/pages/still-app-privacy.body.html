Still App Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026. This policy covers Still, the photo editor for Android (package com.layerbit.still). It is separate from the general Layerbit website privacy policy, which covers this website's own advertising, analytics and cookies; none of that applies to the app, which contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and sets no cookies. In short: Still has no internet permission, so your pictures cannot leave your phone.

No internet, by design

Still does not declare Android's INTERNET permission, so the app cannot open a network connection at all. Nothing you edit, and nothing about you or your phone, is sent anywhere by the app. There are no servers, no accounts and no cloud storage.

What stays on your phone

  • Pictures you open. Still reads only the pictures and PDFs you choose through Android's photo picker, file picker or share sheet. Each is copied into the app's private storage and edited there; your original is never changed.
  • Projects and history. Your edits, undo history, snapshots and saved recipes are stored in the app's private storage on the phone.
  • Exports. A saved picture is written only where you choose to save it. Sharing hands a copy to the app you pick, and that app's own policy applies to it.
  • Faces and subjects. Tools such as "Hide every face" and background removal analyse the picture on the phone, using Android's built-in face finder and Still's own code. No picture or result is sent anywhere.
  • Notifications. Used only to show the progress of a batch while it runs in the background.
  • Crash log. If the app crashes, a log is written to its private storage. It stays there unless you choose to send it from Settings, by email, yourself.

What Still does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no crash reports sent off the phone.
  • No access to your whole photo library, your camera, microphone, location or contacts.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, because none is collected.

Deleting your data

Everything Still keeps is on your phone. Removing a project in the app, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling Still deletes it. Pictures you saved are yours to keep or delete.

Children's privacy

Still is a general-purpose photo editor, not directed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone; it has no account or profile to collect it into.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the date at the top. Still will not gain network access without this policy saying so first.
