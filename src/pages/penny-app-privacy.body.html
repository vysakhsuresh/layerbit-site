Penny App Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026. This policy covers Penny, the money tracker for Android (package app.penny). It is separate from the general Layerbit website privacy policy, which covers this website's own advertising, analytics and cookies; none of that applies to the app, which contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and sets no cookies. In short: your money data lives on your phone, encrypted; anything that leaves it is encrypted first, and only when you turn that feature on.

On your phone, encrypted

Accounts, transactions, budgets, bills, goals, people and notes are stored in an encrypted database on the phone. Its key is protected by the phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore). Penny works fully offline; no account is needed to use it.

Permissions and what they are for

  • Internet. Used only for the optional features below and to fetch public exchange rates. Penny works without it.
  • Notifications. To show bill reminders and alerts you have set.
  • Fingerprint or face (biometrics). Only to unlock Penny when you turn on the app lock. Your biometric data never reaches Penny; Android only tells it yes or no.
  • Google Play billing. Only if you choose to leave a tip. Payment is handled by Google Play; Penny never sees your card details.
  • Reading payment notifications (optional). If you switch this on in Android's settings, Penny reads notifications from your payment and bank apps to suggest transactions. It reads only the apps for your region and any apps you pick; it never reads SMS, and nothing it reads leaves the phone.

Penny has no camera, microphone, contacts or location permission. Scanning a receipt uses the camera app you choose, voice entry uses your phone's speech service, and picking a contact uses Android's contact picker, which gives Penny only the one contact you choose. Receipt text is read on the phone.

Optional features that use the internet

  • Exchange rates. Public reference rates are downloaded from the European Central Bank. The request contains nothing about you.
  • Sync between your devices and shared budgets. Off until you turn it on. Your data is encrypted on the phone with keys only your devices hold (end-to-end encryption) before it is sent, so our servers store only encrypted data they cannot read. Your recovery phrase never leaves your device.
  • Bank connections. Available only with sync on and only for banks you connect. Transactions pass through our connector service to reach your phone; the service keeps only a marker of what was last fetched, not your transactions.
  • Email forwarding. If you create a forwarding address, mail sent to it is encrypted to your phone as soon as it arrives and is readable only there.
  • Community merchant names (opt-in). If you join, Penny shares a merchant's name with the category you chose, without amounts, dates or account details. A suggestion is shown to others only after several different people agree.
  • Backups. Backups you save are encrypted with a recovery phrase you write down. Exports are saved only where you choose.
  • Webhooks. If you set one up, Penny sends the event you choose to your own secure (https) address and nowhere else.

What Penny does not do

  • No advertising, no lending or credit offers, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no crash reports sent off the phone.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • Usage counts, if you turn them on, stay on the phone and contain counts only, never amounts, payees or notes.

Deleting your data

Deleted items go to Penny's Trash and are removed for good after 30 days, or straight away if you choose. You can delete a whole profile, or everything, from Settings. Uninstalling Penny removes all data on the phone. If you used sync, deleting your sync account from the app removes your encrypted data from our servers.

Children's privacy

Penny is a general-purpose money tracker, not directed at children. A parent can keep a separate profile for a child on their own phone; that data stays on the phone like everything else.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the date at the top. Penny will not send any new kind of data off the phone without this policy saying so first.
