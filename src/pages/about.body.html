About Layerbit

Layerbit is a developer-first platform dedicated to offering a curated suite of tools and services that make everyday tasks simpler, faster, and more efficient. We specialize in browser-based utilities that require zero installation, ensuring that high-quality tools are accessible to coders, freelancers, and tech enthusiasts instantly.

How Layerbit Started

Layerbit began as a personal collection of small scripts built to solve recurring, everyday frustrations in a developer's workflow — decoding a stray JWT, reformatting a messy SQL query, or converting a CSV export without opening a spreadsheet program. What started as single-purpose command-line snippets was rebuilt into a unified, browser-based toolkit so that any engineer could reach for the same utilities instantly, from any device, without installing dependencies or granting a third-party service access to their data.

Security-First Philosophy

At Layerbit, we believe that your data should remain yours. Most of our tools operate entirely on the client-side, meaning sensitive information—such as JWT payloads, SQL queries, or JSON logs—is processed locally in your browser and never sent to our servers. This decentralized approach provides an ironclad layer of privacy and security that traditional cloud-based tools simply cannot match. The handful of tools that do require a network call — like BreachAlert or the Malicious URL Scanner — are clearly labeled with a "Cloud API" badge so you always know exactly how your input is handled before you use it.

What We Build

Our catalog spans four broad categories: security and inspection utilities (JWT decoding, breach checks, malicious URL scanning), data conversion and encoding tools (Base64, CSV to JSON, YAML to JSON, URL encoding), code formatting and visualization tools (SQL formatting, JSON validation, regex testing, text diffing), and workflow automation utilities (cron scheduling, log analysis, and LayerLink screen sharing). Beyond the free browser tools, Layerbit also takes on custom engineering work — API automation, ERP systems, mobile app development, static site deployments, and portfolio design — for teams that need something built around their exact requirements rather than a generic template.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower the global developer community with smart digital solutions that are easy to use, beautifully designed, and built for real-world engineering challenges. We are constantly expanding our ecosystem based on user feedback to include the exact utilities that matter most to your daily workflow. Every tool we ship is held to the same bar: it should be faster than searching for an alternative, safer than pasting sensitive data into an unknown website, and genuinely useful for the engineers who rely on it daily.
