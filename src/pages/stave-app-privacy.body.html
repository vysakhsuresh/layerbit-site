Stave App Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026. This policy covers Stave, the audio studio for Android (package com.layerbit.stave). It is separate from the general Layerbit website privacy policy, which covers this website's own advertising, analytics and cookies; none of that applies to the app, which contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and sets no cookies. In short: Stave has no internet permission, so your recordings cannot leave your phone.

No internet, by design

Stave does not declare Android's INTERNET permission, so the app cannot open a network connection at all. Nothing you record or edit, and nothing about you or your phone, is sent anywhere by the app. There are no servers, no accounts and no cloud storage. The models that clean voices, separate songs and transcribe speech run on the phone; on Google Play they are installed with the app by Google Play itself.

The microphone

Stave asks for the microphone only to record, to measure your audio round trip and to run the tuner and live voice effects, and only after you ask it to. While the mic is open, Android shows its own indicator and Stave shows a notification. What the microphone hears is written only into your project on the phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Sounds you open. Stave reads only the audio and video files you choose through Android's file picker or share sheet. Each is copied into the app's private storage and edited there; your original is never changed.
  • Projects and history. Your recordings, edits, transcripts, beats, undo history and settings are stored in the app's private storage on the phone.
  • Exports. An export is written only where you choose to save it. Sharing hands a copy to the app you pick, and that app's own policy applies to it.
  • Notifications. Used only to show that recording, playback or a long job is running in the background.
  • Crash log. If the app crashes, a log is written to its private storage. It stays there unless you choose to share it from Settings yourself.

What Stave does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no crash reports sent off the phone.
  • No access to your whole music library, your camera, location or contacts.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, because none is collected.

Deleting your data

Everything Stave keeps is on your phone. Deleting a project in the app, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling Stave removes it. Files you exported are yours to keep or delete.

Children's privacy

Stave is a general-purpose audio editor, not directed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone; it has no account or profile to collect it into.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the date at the top. Stave will not gain network access without this policy saying so first.
