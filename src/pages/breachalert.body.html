Zero-Knowledge K-Anonymity Architecture

How do we verify your credentials without actually seeing them? Layerbit engineers a strict, zero-trust validation pipeline that guarantees your sensitive data never touches a backend server.

  • Client-Side Cryptography: The moment you type, Layerbit leverages your browser's native Web Crypto API to instantly scramble your password into a secure SHA-1 hash. The raw text never leaves the input box.
  • K-Anonymity Prefixing: We slice off just the first 5 characters of that hash and transmit them to the global threat registry. Because millions of different passwords share those exact same 5 starting characters, your specific password remains perfectly hidden in the crowd.
  • Air-Gapped Resolution: The registry replies with a massive list of compromised suffixes. Layerbit's engine scans this list locally on your own hardware to find a match. Your complete hash is never transmitted across the network.
