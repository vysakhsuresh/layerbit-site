Squish App Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026. This policy covers the Squish video editor for Android (package com.squish.app) specifically. It is separate from the general Layerbit website privacy policy, which covers this website's own advertising, analytics and cookies; none of that applies to the app, which contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and sets no cookies. In short: your videos, photos, sound and projects never leave your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your media and projects. Everything you edit, every project and every file you export is read, processed and stored on your device only. Squish has no servers, no accounts and no cloud storage, and it never uploads a video, photo, recording or project.
  • Microphone. Used only when you record a voiceover or ask for automatic captions. Speech recognition runs on the phone.
  • Camera. Used only when you record a clip from inside Squish.
  • Photos, videos and music. Read only so that you can add them to an edit.
  • Notifications. Used only to show the progress of an export, which runs in a foreground service so it can finish while the screen is off.

Optional online features

Squish works fully offline. A switch in Settings, off by default, allows a few optional features to use the internet. When you use one, only what that feature needs is sent, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS):

  • Translate captions sends the caption text you choose to translate to MyMemory (api.mymemory.translated.net).
  • Free stock footage and music sends the search words you type to the Internet Archive (archive.org).
  • Online fonts sends the name of the font family to Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com).
  • Speech and voice models are downloaded from Hugging Face and GitHub; nothing about you is sent.

Squish does not attach any identifier, account or device ID to these requests. Those services receive your device's IP address as part of any internet connection, and their own privacy policies apply to it.

What Squish does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no crash reports sent off the phone.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Deleting your data

Everything Squish keeps is on your phone. Deleting a project in the app, or uninstalling Squish, removes it. Exported videos are in your gallery and are yours to keep or delete.

Children's privacy

Squish is a general-purpose video editor, not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone; the app has no account or profile to collect it into.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the date at the top. A change that sends anything new off the phone would be described here before it ships, not afterwards.
