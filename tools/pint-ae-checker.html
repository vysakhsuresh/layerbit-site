What is PINT-AE?

PINT-AE is the UAE's e-invoicing data format. Instead of a PDF or Excel invoice that a computer can't reliably read, PINT-AE is a structured XML file — built on the international "Peppol" e-invoicing network — that tax systems and accounting software can process automatically.

The UAE Federal Tax Authority (FTA) has published technical guidance requiring businesses to eventually issue invoices in this format instead of plain PDFs, spreadsheets, or paper.

Do I Need This?

If you invoice customers in the UAE and are VAT-registered, yes — eventually. The rollout is phased, so most small businesses have time to prepare:

  • From July 2026 — voluntary. You can start early to get comfortable.
  • From January 2027 — mandatory for businesses with annual revenue of AED 50 million or more.
  • From July 2027 — mandatory for all other VAT-registered businesses.

Why UAE E-Invoicing Compliance Matters

Most UAE SMEs currently issue invoices as PDFs or Excel sheets. That works for a human reading the invoice, but it has zero structured compliance value once e-invoicing becomes mandatory — tax authorities and trading partner systems need machine-readable XML, not a picture of an invoice. Getting familiar with the PINT-AE format now, well before your mandatory date, avoids a scramble later.
