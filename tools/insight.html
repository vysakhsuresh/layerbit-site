Enterprise Applications & Methodology

Layerbit Device Insight utilizes native HTML5 APIs, WebGL rendering contexts, and IP routing databases to generate a comprehensive profile of your machine's footprint on the web. This diagnostic telemetry is critical for several enterprise security scenarios.

Anti-Fraud & Payment Security

Detect high-risk transaction attempts by identifying mismatching geospatial data, VPN exit nodes, or devices flagged as bots/emulators using hardware fingerprinting.

OSINT Investigation & Compliance

Gather critical device telemetry during authorized red-teaming exercises to verify target authenticity or enforce strict geographic compliance (Geo-fencing) standards.

Authentication & Ad Fraud

Bypass standard browser spoofing layers to identify unique GPU hardware via WebGL extraction, essential for ad verification and identifying sophisticated botnets.
